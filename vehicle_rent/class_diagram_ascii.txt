Notes on relationships
-----------------------
- Vehicle is abstract; Car, Bike, and Van inherit from it and override
  calculateRentalCost() -- this is the inheritance / polymorphism shown
  by the hollow-triangle arrow (^) pointing up to Vehicle.
- PaymentProcessor is an interface; CardPayment and UpiPayment implement
  it, shown the same way (^ pointing up to the interface).
- Customer has a 1-to-many association with Rental: one customer (1) can
  have zero or many rentals (0..*) over time, stored in rentalHistory.
- Rental is composed of a Customer and a Vehicle (cannot exist without
  either) and depends on PaymentProcessor to confirm payment before the
  rental is finalized -- shown as a plain box (dependency, not ownership).
- Rental produces an Invoice once completeRental() runs.
- RentalService orchestrates all of the above; it holds no business logic
  of its own, only coordination (add/register/rent/return).
